## Method limitations

- Dates and organizations are fictional; assumed reporting deadlines are *planning examples*, not actual notices submitted.
- The regulatory matrix lists **selected checkpoints**, not a legally comprehensive compliance matrix.
- DORA major incident classification **cannot** be inferred solely from a service outage or security buzzword; full RTS details, definitions and thresholds matter.
- The DORA ICT contractual register CSV is deliberately **not equivalent** to the official Annex templates mandated by Regulation 2024/2956.
- GDPR notification involves the rights and freedoms of people, not only numerical transaction impact. Separate legal awareness and risk assessment must be documented.
- Local competent authority, licensed entity category, exceptions and procedural changes require confirmation before any real-world use.
