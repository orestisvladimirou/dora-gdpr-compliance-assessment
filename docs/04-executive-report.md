# 04 — Simulated executive compliance assessment

**Audience:** Fictional board risk committee of Aurora Payment Services. **Date:** 5 November 2026. Hypothetical December 2026 incidents are modeled in advance. No real board reviewed or approved this report.

## Summary

The v0.1 exercise maps **19 selected regulatory checkpoints**: **11 DORA-related** and **8 GDPR-related**, comprising **nine Gap**, **eight Partial**, and **two Not verified** assessments. These are internal fictional *readiness observations*, **not findings of legal violations**.

Three distinct incident exercises were constructed:

1. INC-001: major ICT scenario under simplified DORA materiality criteria **and** potentially reportable GDPR personal-data breach, with different awareness clocks.
2. INC-002: limited ICT slowdown without supplied evidence for a major DORA incident or personal-data breach.
3. INC-003: reportable personal-data breach on assumed risk facts but **no major ICT incident** under the simplified DORA scenario.

## Priority decisions requested

- Validate entity, function and jurisdictional regulatory scope before relying on any deadlines or register templates.
- Assign incident commander, legal/regulatory reporting lead and privacy/DPO owners and establish approval/backup procedures.
- Independently verify affected-customer percentages, critical-service impact, exposure of personal data, harm likelihood and recognition timestamps.
- Test practical DORA classification and GDPR assessment checklists, including edge cases where classification is delayed or breach awareness differs from ICT detection.
- Complete operational resilience exercises and the official DORA ICT contractual register with the appropriate ITS templates.

All nine remediation items remain **Open - fictional**. No contract, reporting procedure, regulator submission, DPO decision or management risk acceptance has been approved.

## Limitation

This is not a legal compliance certification or DORA/GDPR legal opinion. The status labels organize a practice work program; requirements can depend on detailed entity classification, authoritative guidance and local regulator procedures.
