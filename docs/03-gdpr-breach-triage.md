# 03 — GDPR personal-data breach response

**Fictional training decisions, not legal judgments or real notifications.**

A breach of personal data can concern confidentiality, availability or integrity, and cannot be equated automatically with all cyber incidents. Under GDPR Article 33, controller notification to a competent supervisory authority is generally required unless the breach is unlikely to create a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, without undue delay and where feasible **within 72 hours of the controller becoming aware**. A processor informs the controller without undue delay. Article 34 generally calls for communication to individuals where **high risk** is likely, subject to its exceptions and factual assessment. A decision not to notify must still be documented where applicable.
