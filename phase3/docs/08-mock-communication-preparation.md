# 08 — Mock regulatory communication preparation

**NO SUBMISSIONS.** This file describes six draft packet records for a fictional December 2026 incident case. It is neither a competent-authority template nor a completed GDPR or DORA form. No actual employees, customers or systems are involved.

## DORA report preparation

The fictional reporting team would prepare an **initial**, **intermediate** and **final** packet for INC-001 only, because this scenario is *assumed* to meet the major-incident gate. Content is instructional:

- **Initial (PKT-301):** identifying details, critical-service and customer impact, first classification, response coordination, current facts and uncertainties.
- **Intermediate (PKT-302):** event chronology, containment, updated impact and restoration progress. Its clock can only be established from an actual submission; this project has none. Phase 2's illustrative submissions must not be mistaken for real reporting.
- **Final (PKT-303):** cause analysis, measurable losses, mitigations, recovery and lessons. A real final template, competent authority requirements and the updated intermediate timeline would need verification.

All packet rows explicitly say **DRAFT - SIMULATED** and **NONE - SIMULATED** for actual submission. The official reporting format and data fields depend on the implementing/technical rules, including [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/301/oj) and [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/302](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/302/oj). The source references are for research, not compliance assurance.

## GDPR record preparation

The fictional DPO prepares two assumed supervisory-authority notification drafts (INC-001 and INC-003) and one provisional **communication to affected individuals** (INC-001). They remain unsigned drafts; they are *not* sent to a regulator or person.

A real GDPR authority notification must examine Article 33(3) content: nature and approximate subjects/records, DPO contact, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed. Individual communication depends on a high-risk decision and Article 34 exceptions. Neither can be concluded solely by this hypothetical questionnaire. A controller and any processor must be identified for the actual processing context.

## Controlled decision rule

The [packet register](../data/mock_communication_packets.csv) records owner, gaps, preparation date, regulatory source and explicit non-submission status. **No recipient address, portal, customer identity, regulator acknowledgement or fabricated certificate appears in the repository.**
