## Evidence/assurance limitations

This is a desktop exercise. There is **no live ICT environment**, regulated business, actual customer record, actual supplier, data subject contact, competent-authority communication, independent auditor, verified ICT contractual register, real board approval or established DORA/GDPR regulatory status. The hypothetical 2026–2027 events are explicitly imagined and are not past events.

A status marked **Gap** in this repository describes a training scenario missing assumed evidence, not a legal finding of violation. DORA major classification must be checked against the full delegated regulation, including exceptions and potential recurrence. GDPR notification depends on reliable knowledge of a personal-data breach and a rights-and-freedoms risk assessment. A filled register or passing Python test does not establish legal compliance.
